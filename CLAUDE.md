# Manhattan Miles: notes for Claude

First-person walking and fighting game on all of Manhattan, street by street (Mark's Vigil map: OpenStreetMap streets, buildings, parks and water, real businesses, the MTA subway): march to walk, high knees to run, steer by turning your shoulders like a steering wheel, walk to real places. Play: https://hungkach.github.io/manhattan-miles/

## Design philosophy: honest reps

Every rep counts, better reps count more, and the scoreboard doesn't lie. The full version lives in `hungkach/body-arcade` CLAUDE.md and wins over any habit here. In short:

- Effort is subjective, output is objective. A tired rep or an unfit player's best rep always works (the floor is the player's own weakest honest warm-up rep).
- Reward scales with absolute output in body units, capped at a safe range. Never re-normalize to the player's best or early-session reps: fitness gains are the progression.
- Outcomes need the reps that earn them; nothing for time spent or showing up, no XP-per-minute grind.
- Records track output across weeks (deepest squat, highest knees, fastest punch, longest bound, best cadence).
- Summaries state facts: no cheering, no praise lines, no scolding, no fatigue commentary. Rest comes from the sport's rhythm.
- Plain tone; humor aims at the world, never the player's effort.

## How this repo works

- `src/index.html` is the game. Edit it, then `npm run build` writes the playable page to `index.html` at the repo root (GitHub Pages serves that). Commit both.
- The 3D city: `src/gl.js` (`MMGL`), inlined by the build; see the 10/23 note below.
- The map: `src/world.js` (`MMWorld`) decodes `data/world.b64` (gzip binary of streets, nodes, building boxes and polygons by 500 m chunk, ground polygons by 1 km tile), `data/biz.json` and `data/subway.json`, all taken from Mark's Vigil prototype artifact; the build inlines them (about 1.2 MB). Game coordinates: x east, z north, meters, origin at Times Square (the data stores z south; world.js flips it). It answers walkable, streetAt, nearestEdge, route (Dijkstra on walkable streets), intersection, buildingAt, groundAt, bizNear, stations. `test/world.test.mjs` runs it in Node.
- `engine/` is a locked copy of rep-engine from `hungkach/body-arcade`; `engine/SOURCE.json` says which commit. Never edit `engine/` here. Engine changes happen in body-arcade, then this game takes them with `node scripts/update-engine.mjs ../body-arcade` when Mark chooses.
- `npm test` must pass before every push; it also fails if `index.html` is out of date with a fresh build.
- No dependencies. Single self-contained HTML file. WebGL 2 for the city (`src/gl.js`), plain canvas for everything else and as the classic fallback.
- `window.__game` exposes state for headless browser checks.
- Update the `BUILD` stamp in src/index.html (date and a few words) with every push; it shows on the title screen so Mark can tell when his phone is showing a cached page.

## Engine contract

- Read `engine.signals()` each frame; subscribe with `engine.on(event)`. Never touch landmarks.
- Touch demo mode (`engine.enable_demo(container)`) must keep the game fully playable; it emits the same events as the camera.
- Engine spec: https://claude.ai/code/artifact/a394f929-94ab-43c0-b06c-4d4b2cc50559

## Hard constraints

- Phone or iPad only, no accessories; living room preset, phone propped about 4 ft away, knees-up framing.
- Fitness first: the game names its S&C payoff, matches a work-to-rest rhythm, sessions 10 to 20 minutes.
- Names stay neutral and fitness oriented.

- 10/30 seasons (Mark: "a seasonal mode: summer, spring, fall, winter (Christmas), with themed atmosphere and decorations"): title pick "Season" (`#season`, `manhattan-miles-season`; Auto follows the date: March to May spring, June to August summer, September to November fall, December to February winter). `SEASONS`, `applySeason`, `SEA`: tree leaves (`drawProp`; winter is snow on bare branches, smaller), park and lawn ground (`MW.GROUND_COL` 1 and 2, both renderers; the 3D city is rebuilt on a change, `GLF.key`), a tint on the look's sky, haze and clouds (`setLook` mixes `SEA.tint`; looks carry an `id`, compared as `L.id === 'golden'`), what drifts through the air over the city (`Weather`, `drawWeather`: snow, falling leaves, petals, fireflies on summer nights; turning sweeps it across the view), sidewalk decorations by the buildings (`deco`: planters in spring, pumpkins lit at night in fall, from their own random draws so lamps and trees stay put), and lines for passers-by and vendors (40% and 50% of the time). - 10/30 the budget (Mark: "if someone is fit and skilled enough, there could theoretically be a mob of 50 high tier enemies; I don't want fixed tiers or groups, all variables"; no cap on a fight's length, no rounds): every encounter is a strength budget from the street rating (`rateTarget`), spent by `buildGroup(T, shape)`; nothing caps the size or the tier. Every opponent has a style (its type; the tier is how many styles share a body, `RATE.tierR` 800 to 1800, the boss 1900) and a rank `f.rk`, any number (`RANK`: each rank is 60 rating points and adds 4% health, 3% guard, 20 ms off the wind-up, 2% quicker thinking, 0.02 head movement (half for thugs) and 0.01 habit reading; bodies never under 0.7, wind-ups never under `FT.minWindupMs` 450, the one floor kept, for reaction time). Group strength adds like Elo strengths (`encRating(members)`), and after the styles are picked every rank moves together until the group comes to the budget exactly. Shapes (`SHAPES`): mob 0.45 (many of one style, `mobN` 3 plus one per 40 points over 1000, mostly thugs while thugs fit at rank 0.5 to 8, then tier 2 and up; 8 or more have a leader of the next style last in line), duel 0.4 (one or two of the hardest style that fits, never thugs), crew 0.15 (mixed styles, `crewN`). Groups line up weakest first. The health bar says the rank band (`rankBand`: Green, plain, Seasoned 3, Hardened 6, Veteran 10, Elite 15, Feared 21). Streaks: 3 clean wins in a row (score 0.85 or more) push the next one 150 harder (`RATE.push`); every loss eases the next as before, and 2 in a row also make the walk to the next one 1.5 times longer. Work to rest: the walk to the next encounter is as long as the last fight, 40 to 120 s (`restGap`, `FT.restS`). Crime centers are waves from one budget (`WAVES`: a mob, a crew, a bigger mob, a duel as the mid-boss, each 30 more, then the boss 150 over the budget at its own rank; rows of four up the street). Scenes stay small crews (`FT.sceneMax` 6; their ranks carry the budget). Openers keep their scripted groups, now with ranks. Only the nearest `FT.drawFoes` 24 opponents are drawn. Simulated: a clean walk climbs from 3 thugs to mobs of 15 to 18 hardened thugs and tier 3 and 4 duels by the 35th fight; at rating 2600 a mob is 40 to 44 Legends; a demo mob of 36 Legends ran at 52 frames a second in classic in headless Chromium.
