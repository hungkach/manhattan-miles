# Manhattan Miles: notes for Claude

First-person walking game on a Midtown grid (12th to 1st Ave, 14th to 59th St) at real block proportions: march to walk, high knees to run, steer by turning your shoulders like a steering wheel, walk to real destinations. Play: https://hungkach.github.io/manhattan-miles/

## How this repo works

- `src/index.html` is the game. Edit it, then `npm run build` writes the playable page to `index.html` at the repo root (GitHub Pages serves that). Commit both.
- `engine/` is a locked copy of rep-engine from `hungkach/body-arcade`; `engine/SOURCE.json` says which commit. Never edit `engine/` here. Engine changes happen in body-arcade, then this game takes them with `node scripts/update-engine.mjs ../body-arcade` when Mark chooses.
- `npm test` must pass before every push; it also fails if `index.html` is out of date with a fresh build.
- No dependencies. Single self-contained HTML file. Plain canvas.
- `window.__game` exposes state for headless browser checks.
- Update the `BUILD` stamp in src/index.html (date and a few words) with every push; it shows on the title screen so Mark can tell when his phone is showing a cached page.

## Engine contract

- Read `engine.signals()` each frame; subscribe with `engine.on(event)`. Never touch landmarks.
- Touch demo mode (`engine.enable_demo(container)`) must keep the game fully playable; it emits the same events as the camera.
- Engine spec: https://claude.ai/code/artifact/a394f929-94ab-43c0-b06c-4d4b2cc50559

## Hard constraints

- Phone or iPad only, no accessories; living room preset, phone propped about 4 ft away, knees-up framing.
- Fitness first: the game names its S&C payoff, matches a work-to-rest rhythm, sessions 10 to 20 minutes.
- Names stay neutral and fitness oriented.

## Working with Mark

- He tests on his phone and often works from mobile: short replies, lead with the answer, committed recommendations.
- Decision logic and pseudocode as Python if/elif/else blocks with instructions in comments.
- No em dashes and no all-caps emphasis in docs or chat; sentence case.

- Playable with touch and camera: free walking on streets and sidewalks (buildings block), steering-wheel torso turn, run, point for a quick 90 degree turn, turn around, destinations, street sign HUD, minimap, summary with S&C payoff.
- Setup modes (`SETUPS`): phone or tablet, upright or sideways (the device's actual orientation picks which), and TV (engine `tv` preset: back camera, ultra-wide when it can; `engine.camera()` says what opened and the spoken placement adapts).
- Voice: the engine is created with `speech: false`; the game's `say()` queue voices everything with 2 s gaps between instructions.
- Movement runs on `engine.locomotion()` in the free model (Mark's call after the TV test: a steering wheel, not rails). Hold a torso turn to keep turning (engine `locomotion.maxTurnRate`, 90 degrees a second at full turn), face front to go straight; marching or high knees set speed (times `T.speedScale`). Self-centering settles you onto a street's direction within 12 degrees when you stop steering. A point is a quick 90 degree turn via `set_facing`; turn around is the engine's +180. Running widens the view and says "Running".
- Fights (`Fight`, `FT` in src/index.html): the first opponent waits about 10 marching steps ahead of the start (`FT.firstAhead`), then every 75 to 110 s of walking an opponent (later sometimes two) waits ahead; reaching them calls `engine.enter_combat({ bearing, open, shooting })`. Opponent AI: guard, telegraphed straight or hook from either hand (`foe.hookSide`; wind-up glow on that hand; the full guard blocks either, and a hook is also blocked by the player's hand on its side up by the ear, `guard_left`/`guard_right`, which Mark found by accident and liked; slip dodges a straight and opens a counter), shot (sprawl stops it), step back (they come back in after 1.2 to 2 s; no marching in, it breaks the fight stance), cover-up (straights do 30% and wear the guard; hooks, uppercuts and body shots go around). Takedowns (engine 0.5.x, Mark's rework): holding the squat (`level_low`) sprawls their shot; a drive up out of it is the shot, and the engine asks `resolveShot` (the `shot` callback in `enter_combat`): braced opponents stuff anything under `FT.td.beatBrace` power (a jump beats it), and a shot timed into a wind-up, hurt, broken guard or stuffed opponent earns a longer ground and pound (`FT.td.gnpMs` 2 to 5 s, a countdown bar); on top, any fast extending punch from either hand is a down strike with no aiming (`gloveHits`, `FT.hit.gnp*`; Mark: targeting on the ground does not matter), then they scramble up. A crouch held `FT.td.braceAfterMs` makes them brace (`brace` state); an uppercut on a braced or shooting opponent does double damage ("Read it!"). Guard breaks (`FT.guard`): head straights into a cover-up wear `foe.guardHp` down; at 0 the guard breaks (`broken`, open for 1.6 s); a guard bar shows while it is worn. Every third opponent is a turtle (`foe.turtle`) who covers up a lot. Punch trade-offs: between moves they drift between close (`FT.rangeClose` 1.6 m) and mid (`FT.rangeMid` 2.3 m) range and step in to punch; straights reach mid range, hooks and uppercuts only land close (`FT.reach`, against the real distance; a whiff says "Too far for a hook"); straights hit softer, hooks hardest, uppercuts always land to the body (`FT.damage`). The HUD tag says the range. Knockout, getting dropped (10 hits) or 3 s of high knees ends it. Drawing is 3D in plain canvas: capsules and spheres projected through the game camera with near clipping and depth sorting (`capsule`, `ball`, `drawPrims`); the opponent's joints ease toward per-state poses (`targetPose`), straights fly at where your head was at the wind-up (`foe.aim`), and a ground shadow anchors them. Your arms are capsules from `engine.arms()` with a wider view-model lens (`drawArms`); a fist tilts up as it extends so a straight at shoulder height ends on their chin (`aimArms`, `armCam`). Punches land two ways, deduplicated per hand (`claim`, `FT.hit.pairMs`): the engine's `strike` events (lock-on), and the glove hitbox (`gloveHits`): a glove moving fast, out in front, that ends over their projected head or torso (`foe.hit`, set in `drawFoe`); body shots get through a cover-up. Hits show a burst, a recoil and a WebAudio thump. A level bar shows squat depth against the shot mark; a squat that is not a shot says why. The first 28 s of each camera fight is recorded (`Fight.clip`, "Save fight clip" on the summary). The camera follows your body (`povUpdate`): slip moves and rolls it, squat drops it, the shot lunges it, top looks down. Touch mode swaps to fight buttons. Summary lists fights, punches landed, takedowns and fight time with the S&C payoff.
- Camera setup: framing and baseline via `engine.calibrate(preset, { moveTest: false })`, then the game's own timed warm-up (march, turn left, turn right; `Warm` in src/index.html) over the engine's `DebugOverlay` (stick figure only; mid-walk a three-finger tap shows it with the signal panel). Each march round is captured (`capture_start('step', round)`) and `apply_capture()` tunes the step thresholds after it, so a round the defaults miss is caught in the next; tuned cues live in the engine profile. The warm-up clip is saved from the end screen. Headless check: `__game.runCamera(() => engine.start_replay(clip))` with a synthetic clip from body-arcade's `test/helpers/synth.mjs`.
- The walk (`SESSION`; no countdown, Mark: an RPG, explore but stay active): `planRoute`/`nextStop` keep one next stop about `SESSION.stopM` away (`pickStop`); `navTick` speaks the turn at the corner ahead (`crossingAhead`, `adviceAt`: straight while there is ground to cover that way, then turn; wrong way says turn around) or "turn here" at the corner you are on, and puts it on the HUD; `sessionTick` counts active time (`G.activeMs`, moving or fighting), says milestones (`SESSION.milestones`, 20 is a full session), nudges after `idleNudgeS` standing still, and runs a 30 s high knees interval every 120 s of walking (a fight or a call resets it). The HUD shows stride from the engine's `stride` signal. Phone menu (`Phone`, `PHONE_ITEMS`): wave left opens, shoulder turn moves (facing is held), wave right picks, wave left closes; the Phone button for touch; the walk pauses. Aftermath: `spawnFoes` adds the person being hassled, `gatherCrowd` a ring of bystanders filming, a beaten opponent may flee (`FT.flee*`), `aftermath` leaves a knocked-out opponent down, cheers and speaks a `LINES` line (blue humor). Police radio (phone menu, `radio`): a call (`CALL_TYPES`) at a corner 350 to 750 m away with a time limit that needs some running (`CALL`); `target()` points directions at the call, random encounters and intervals wait, arriving (`callTick`) spawns the fight there, and after a win two officers walk up (`officer` people, `OFFICER_LINES`). Police are allies: generic uniforms, no department names, jokes on the player. Pedestrians (`Peds`, `pedTick`): up to 8 on the sidewalks; a right-hand wave (`greet`, touch Wave button) stops the nearest one ahead, who waves back with a `PED_LINES` line. Quick start: a warm-up that saw marching sets `manhattan-miles-warmed-<setup>`; next time `quickPlan` (one march round) runs unless "Full warm-up" is ticked, and a miss falls back to the full rounds.
