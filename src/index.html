    Manhattan Miles

    Midtown, 14th to 59th Street, avenue by avenue. Walk it for real.

    • March in place to walk; march faster to walk faster, high knees to run
    • Point left or right for a quick turn onto the cross street
    • Turn your shoulders to steer, like a steering wheel; face front to go straight
    • Both hands overhead to turn around
    • Opponents wait on some blocks: guard up and punch for real, slip their punches, squat and drive up for a takedown, high knees to escape. Your view follows your slips and level changes.

    Explore the city: there is always a next stop, the game talks you through each turn, calls high knees intervals and nudges you when you stand around. Twenty active minutes is a full session. Wave your left hand to open the phone menu.
